# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-22

Initial public site and operations backend for Loaded Valet Trash.

- Property-manager site for Valdosta and Lowndes County, with proposal, missed-pickup, and review forms
- Owner, manager, and porter admin: pipeline, properties, roster, routes, porter check-off, issues, billing, moderation, settings, and audit log
- Authenticator sign-in, migrations, an owner-only seed, and a local-only demo seed
- Test suite for auth, roles, billing math, route estimates, review moderation, and public forms
